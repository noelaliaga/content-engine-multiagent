# Context — Quillfern Plant Co. (@quillfern.example)

> **SYNTHETIC CLIENT.** Quillfern Plant Co. is an invented brand created for this repository.
> Every name, number and observation below is fictional. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental.

## 1. Identity and offer
- FACT: small direct-to-consumer houseplant brand aimed at beginners; 18,400 followers (fictional number).
- FACT: sells a monthly beginner care-kit subscription: one easy plant, a soil mix and access to a plant-care chat.
- HYPOTHESIS: most followers follow for the satisfying repotting videos, not because they want to keep plants alive.

## 2. Real business model
- FACT: recurring revenue from the subscription; one-off plant sales are a small share.
- HYPOTHESIS: churn is highest in month two, when the first plant struggles.

## 3. Visible funnel
- FACT: reels -> profile -> link in bio -> subscription page.
- FACT: the DM keyword RESCUE sends a free care checklist.
- HYPOTHESIS: the checklist DM is rarely mentioned in reels, so the bridge from reach to offer is weak.

## 4. Reach engine
- FACT: repotting and propagation reels with ASMR audio get the most reach.
- FACT: "rescued plant" before/after transformations also perform well.

## 5. Buyer persona
- Renters in their late 20s and 30s who have already killed at least one plant and feel "bad with plants".
- Pains: overwatering guilt, not knowing what light their flat has, fear of wasting money.

## 6. Constraints and tone
- Keep the calm, slightly nerdy ASMR style that drives reach.
- Tone: warm, reassuring, never shaming.
- Never promise that a plant "cannot die" or any guaranteed outcome.

## 7. Open notes and hypotheses
- HYPOTHESIS: subscriber testimonials exist in DMs but are not used in the feed.
- HYPOTHESIS: care education content would reduce month-two churn.
