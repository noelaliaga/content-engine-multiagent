# KB — Hooks

Generic opening mechanics, reusable across niches and not tied to any specific business. The Growth Strategist and Content Ideation pick the ones that fit the pillars; the Brand Analyst cites them when describing `viral_patterns_observed`.

- **POV / emotional narrative** — the viewer takes the perspective of a specific moment ("POV: you just…").
- **Result reveal** — show the outcome first, then explain how it happened.
- **Contrarian** — contradict a common belief in the niche to create friction and stop the scroll.
- **Open loop** — open a question or tension that only resolves if the viewer keeps watching or reading.
- **Social proof** — a testimonial, result or case from a real third party (customer, student) as evidence.
- **Teach-a-piece** — teach one useful, self-contained fragment of a larger process.
- **Before/after** — explicit contrast between an initial and a final state.
- **Common mistake** — point out a frequent mistake in the niche and correct it.
- **Myth vs reality** — take apart a widespread false idea.
- **Real case** — narrate a concrete, verifiable situation rather than a hypothetical example.
- **Transformation** — an arc of personal or business change over time.
- **Lifestyle with a bridge to the offer** — aspirational content that explicitly connects to what is sold, not aesthetics with no commercial destination.
- **Process/reward** — show the effort and the reward that follows, reinforcing cause and effect.
