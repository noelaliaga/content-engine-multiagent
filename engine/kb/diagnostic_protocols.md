# KB — Commercial diagnostic protocols

Diagnostic criteria that govern how the Brand Analyst detects gaps and how the Growth Strategist prioritises pillars. They are a way of thinking applied to content analysis, not rules for a specific niche.

- **Diagnose commercial leaks first** — before proposing anything, identify where real opportunity is being lost, not where things "could be improved" in the abstract.
- **Separate reach, conversion, positioning, offer and trust** — a misclassified gap leads to the wrong fix; label every gap precisely.
- **Do not break the engine that already works** — every recommendation starts from what already produces results instead of replacing it with a generic system.
- **Look for missing social proof** — especially when the offer carries perceived purchase risk.
- **Look for a missing content-to-offer bridge** — reach content with no clear path to conversion is a leak, even if reach is good.
- **Look for excess top-of-funnel content** — a feed dominated by emotional or aspirational content with no MOFU/BOFU layers has a conversion ceiling even as reach grows.
- **Every piece needs an intent** — reach, nurture or convert. A piece without a declared intent should not be produced.
- **Never promise guaranteed results** — any specific, guaranteed outcome is a `claim_safety` violation and must be flagged in QA.
- **Adapt patterns, never copy** — extract the mechanics (hook, structure, emotion) of an observed pattern and apply them to this brand; copying a third party's script or format verbatim is not acceptable.
- **One strong piece beats five generic ones** — prioritise depth and specificity over production volume.
