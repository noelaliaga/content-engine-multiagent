# KB — Content patterns and strategy

Structural concepts the Growth Strategist uses to build `content_pillars` and `content_mix`, and that Content QA uses to judge `pillar_fit` and `offer_connection`.

- **TOFU / MOFU / BOFU** — top, middle and bottom of the funnel. TOFU = reach and discovery; MOFU = trust and education; BOFU = direct conversion. A healthy feed has all three layers, not only TOFU.
- **Reach vs conversion** — more reach does not mean more sales without a bridge to the offer; balance both goals explicitly in each piece.
- **Content-to-offer bridge** — an explicit mechanism (CTA, keyword, DM, link) connecting a reach piece to the real offer. Without a bridge, reach does not convert.
- **Quality over quantity** — one well-executed, specific piece beats five generic pieces in real impact on the business goal.
- **80% adapted proven patterns / 20% experiments** — most content uses mechanics already validated in the niche, adapted to the brand; a minority tests new angles.
- **Authority** — content that demonstrates judgement and knowledge, not only results or aesthetics.
- **Social proof** — real customer cases; especially important when the purchase carries perceived risk.
- **Education** — content that teaches a real concept or process, building trust before asking for the sale.
- **Objections** — content that anticipates and answers the buyer's typical doubts or fears.
- **CTA** — an explicit call to action consistent with the funnel stage (not every piece should ask for the sale).
